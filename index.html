01 — LEARN

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers pretend to be trusted people or organizations to trick users into giving away sensitive information.

Attackers may try to steal passwords, banking information, personal data, or login credentials.

📧

Email Phishing

Fake emails designed to trick users into clicking malicious links or sharing information.

📱

Smishing

Phishing attacks delivered through SMS or text messages.

🎣

Spear Phishing

Targeted phishing attacks designed for a specific person or organization.

02 — SPOT THE PHISHING

Realistic Phishing Scenarios

📧 Scenario 1 — Email Phishing ⚠️ SUSPICIOUS
📧 Suspicious Email ⚠️ SUSPICIOUS

From: security@paypa1-support.com

Subject: Urgent: Your account will be suspended!

Dear Customer,

We detected unusual activity on your account. Verify your account immediately to avoid suspension.

🚩

Fake Sender

The domain uses "paypa1" instead of the real company name.

🚩

Urgent Language

The message tries to create panic and pressure the user to act quickly.

🚩

Unexpected Request

An unexpected request to verify an account should be checked carefully.

Why is this suspicious?

This message uses a fake-looking sender address, urgent language, and an unexpected account verification request. These are common warning signs of phishing.

📱 Scenario 2 — Smishing ⚠️ SUSPICIOUS
💬 Messages

Unknown Sender

Your bank account has been temporarily locked. Confirm your information within 24 hours to restore access.

🚩

Unknown Sender

The message comes from an unexpected number.

🚩

Time Pressure

The 24-hour deadline is designed to make you act without checking.

🚩

Account Request

Never provide sensitive information through an unexpected message.

Why is this suspicious?

The message comes from an unknown sender and creates pressure by giving a short deadline. It also asks the user to confirm an account unexpectedly.

🌐 Scenario 3 — Fake Website ⚠️ FAKE LOGIN PAGE
🔒 secure-bank-login.example
🏦 SecureBank

Sign In

This is a simulated login page for training.

🚩

Suspicious Domain

Always check the website address before entering login information.

🚩

Fake Login Page

Attackers may create pages that look like trusted websites.

🚩

Credential Theft

Fake login pages can be designed to steal usernames and passwords.

Why is this suspicious?

The website uses a suspicious domain and presents a login form that could be used to steal credentials. Always check the website address before entering sensitive information.

03 — PROTECT YOURSELF

Stay Safe From Phishing

01

Check the Sender

Carefully examine the sender's email address before trusting a message.

02

Don't Rush

Urgent messages are often designed to make you react without checking.

03

Check Links

Hover over links and verify the destination before clicking.

04

Use MFA

Multi-factor authentication provides an additional layer of account protection.

05

Report Suspicious Messages

Report phishing attempts instead of interacting with them.
